Independent research program

Independent, evidence-led research on financial models, market regimes, institutions, and investor decisions.

The lab examines how quantitative methods behave when estimates leave sample, correlations change sign, liquidity deteriorates, and institutional constraints reshape market transmission.

Empirical investigations, historical synthesis, mathematical notes, and quantitative models connect every conclusion to its data vintage, uncertainty, identification limits, and reproducible implementation. Market relationships are treated as conditional structures—not permanent laws.
