The Lab

About the lab

Mission

Investigate how financial models, market regimes, institutions, and history shape investor decisions.

Plain-language conclusions are published with reproducible evidence, explicit uncertainty, and strict causal boundaries. The surface remains accessible; the research becomes more technical as the decision requires.

Questions

Portfolio construction, market risk, diversification, factor exposure, derivatives, numerical error, and the regimes that condition those relationships.

Outputs

Plain-language answers, empirical studies, interactive models, mathematical notes, frozen datasets, and reproducible source artifacts.

Evidence standard

Falsifiable claims, versioned data, theoretical anchors, uncertainty estimates, failure criteria, limitations, and public revisions.

Boundary

The site is educational. Frozen histories do not bound future markets, illustrative inputs are not forecasts, and nothing is investment advice.

Publication model

Quantitative Markets & Institutions Lab publishes financial-model research in layers. Each topic begins with a question and a short answer. Evidence, decision implications, limitations, mathematical foundations, datasets, tests, and source code follow in increasing technical depth.

Scientific voice

Claims are stated impersonally and evaluated against declared criteria. Supported, rejected, and revised results receive the same provenance standard. Interpretation remains conditional on the model, data vintage, test design, and estimated error.

Regime research boundary

History supplies context; the test determines what survives

Economic, political, and institutional developments enter the lab only when they define a transmission mechanism, measurable condition, or explicit scenario. A historical episode is not causal evidence by itself, and a compelling narrative cannot substitute for identification.

Measured here

Results generated by declared data, transformations, tests, uncertainty estimates, and source artifacts.

Established context

Historical facts and mechanisms supported by primary research and authoritative institutional records.

Interpretive implication

A bounded decision consequence that remains separate from the evidence supporting the reported result.

All conclusions are conditional on stated models, data vintages, and evaluation designs.
