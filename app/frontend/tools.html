Option Pricing Laboratory

Run Black–Scholes–Merton and a Cox–Ross–Rubinstein tree side by side. Change spot, strike, maturity, rate, dividend yield, and volatility; observe price and Greek sensitivity.

Useful for
No-arbitrage pricing, sensitivity, numerical convergence
Boundary
European exercise and simplified market dynamics

Interactive5–10 minutes

Efficient Frontier Explorer

Sample twenty thousand long-only portfolios over six illustrative asset classes and examine minimum-variance, maximum-Sharpe, and equal-weight answers.

Useful for
Risk–return geometry, constraints, assumption sensitivity
Boundary
Illustrative moments are not forecasts or recommendations
